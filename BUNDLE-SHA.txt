Content identity: the git commit hashes themselves are the identifiers —
every git object is content-addressed, so a successful clone of the bundle
at the reported HEAD hash IS the integrity proof. The delivery report
(parent agent's handoff) carries the HEAD hash and the .b64 SHA256
(computed AFTER the final bundle was cut).
